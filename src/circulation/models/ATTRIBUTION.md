This project uses the adult male BodyParts3D 4.0 reference geometry distributed
by human-atlas. The meshes were rescaled and regrouped into GLB assets. Changes
include retaining vessel branches and smaller bones, separating pulmonary and
systemic vessels, removing liver surfaces from the vascular group, including the
ventricular wall and brain, and replacing display materials.

The lung outer envelopes and animated blood-flow centerlines are simplified
educational additions made by this project. They are not measured anatomy or a
physiological simulation. The underlying open dataset is independent of Complete
Anatomy; no proprietary Complete Anatomy assets are included.
