# Solar System texture attribution

Planetary maps by **Solar System Scope / INOVE**, distributed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Source and license statement: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

The source describes the maps as NASA elevation/imagery based, adjusted against
spacecraft photographs, with enhanced colors and fictional filling of unmapped
regions. They are educational composite maps, not live imagery or wholly
unaltered natural-color photographs.

Files are bundled locally for reliable loading. All maps are the source's 2K
versions. Earth normal and specular maps were converted from TIFF to PNG without
intentional content changes. Source filenames:

Other small-moon surfaces are procedural illustrations. Geometric flattening,
small-scale surface relief, transient cloud features and orbital positions are
not intended to reproduce a specific observation or current ephemeris.

Planet data references:

- https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/
- https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/
- https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/seasons/en/

Equatorial diameter is used consistently for planet size comparison. Distances
and motion are compressed in the orbit overview. Planet diameters share one
scale in size-comparison mode; the Sun is excluded from that comparison.
